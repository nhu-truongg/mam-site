Privacy policy

Effective: 14 November 2026

Mầm is a personal journaling app. In short: what you write stays on your device. Mầm does not collect, sell or share any of your data.

Mầm collects no data

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • Mầm has no server. Nothing you write is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The developer cannot see or access anything you write in Mầm.

Where your data is kept

Your lessons, check-ins, notes, points and badges, received signals, weight (if you turn it on), your name, your cat's name and your settings are stored in Mầm's private storage on your own phone. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also delete everything at any time in Settings → Your data → Delete all data.

If you use your iPhone's iCloud Backup or back up your phone to a computer, iOS may include app data, Mầm's included, in that backup. Apple does this according to your settings and Apple's own policy.

iCloud sync (when you choose)

iCloud sync is off by default. Once you turn it on, each time you tap “Sync now” Mầm merges the copy in your own iCloud Drive into this phone, then saves a full backup of your notebook (the same format as “Export data”) and a list of deleted items (ids and dates only, no content). Mầm never syncs in the background. Apple keeps this data under Apple's own policy; there is no Mầm server in between, and the developer cannot read it. When you turn sync off you choose to keep or remove the copy in iCloud; “Delete all data” removes it too.

Export and import

When you choose Export data, Mầm creates a JSON or CSV file and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where the file goes (Files, AirDrop, email…). From then on the file is outside Mầm and is kept under the policy of wherever you put it. Import data only reads a file you pick yourself.

Sharing and saving pictures

When you share a picture of a signal or a quote, Mầm opens the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. If you choose to save it to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add photos. Mầm adds only the picture you chose and never reads your photo library.

Face ID and passcode

If you turn on App lock, Mầm asks iOS to check Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. iOS does the check on the device; Mầm only receives “yes” or “no” and never receives or stores face, fingerprint or passcode data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your phone, not sent from any server. By default they do not show a lesson's title or content; you can turn on “Show content in notifications” in Settings. When you check in right from a notification, the answer is saved in Mầm on your phone like any other check-in.

Home-screen widget

So the widget can show something, Mầm copies a little information into storage shared between the app and its widget (an App Group) on the same phone: the date, the day's signal and saying, a tip, how many reminders are waiting today, and the theme. The widget never shows a lesson's title.

Sending feedback

When you tap Send feedback in Settings, Mầm opens your Mail app with a new, empty message. At the bottom is one small line: the Mầm version, the iOS version and the language, nothing from your journal. You can read and edit it, and the message is only sent if you send it yourself. Messages you send are used only to reply to you and to improve Mầm.

Support phone numbers

“Need support?” lists emergency phone numbers. Tapping one opens the Phone app; Mầm records nothing about the call.

Children

Mầm collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

Mầm's website uses no cookies, no analytics and loads nothing from elsewhere. The host (GitHub Pages) may keep technical access logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's own policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Mầm ever adds a feature that sends data off your phone, this policy will say so before that feature ships.
