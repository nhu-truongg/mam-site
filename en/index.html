Mầm

Write it down, grow gently, little by little.

Mầm (“sprout” in Vietnamese) is a small notebook for the things you want to change and the lessons life teaches you, with gentle reminders to try a different way. No streaks, no blame, no account.

What Mầm helps with

Change usually comes from very small tries. Mầm keeps them for you, and never takes away what you have learned.

Write down a lesson

What happened, what you took from it, and one line for next time: “Next time I'll try…” Only the title is required; write the rest when you like.

Replying to messages while upset What I learned Things I write in anger are usually things I regret. Next time I'll try… waiting ten minutes before I reply.

Honest check-ins

You don't have to get it right every time. Even slipping into the old way is a check-in worth making.

  • I tried the new way
  • I'm trying a little
  • This didn't come up today
  • I slipped into the old way

A look you like

Three themes: Grassy hill, Black cat (with a cat you name yourself) and Pressed flowers. Light, dark or match device, large text, Vietnamese and English.

iCloud sync, when you choose

Off by default. When you turn it on and tap “Sync now”, Mầm merges your notebook with a copy in your own iCloud. Nothing syncs in the background, and there is no Mầm server in between.

How it works

A few seconds a day is enough.

  1. Write something down

    A habit you want to change or a lesson you just learned, with what you'll try next time.

  2. Add a reminder, if you like

    Pick a time and the days. Mầm reminds you gently and keeps out of your quiet hours.

  3. Check in honestly

    On Today, with “Quick check-in” for every waiting lesson, or right from a notification. Missed a day? Check in for it later.

  4. Watch yourself grow

    Plants in My corner grow, Mầm points go up, and the calendar keeps every day you've walked through.

Truly private

What you write in Mầm is yours. Your data stays on your device.

  • No account, no sign-in. Open the app and start.
  • Your data stays on your device. Mầm has no server; the developer cannot see anything you write.
  • No analytics, no tracking. No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • App lock with Face ID or your device passcode, and content hidden in the app switcher.
  • Discreet notifications. By default they never show a lesson's title or content, even on the lock screen.
  • Your data, in your hands. Export it (JSON, CSV) to keep, import it on a new phone, or delete everything at any time.

Quick questions

Does Mầm cost anything?

Mầm is free, with no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

Mầm is for iPhone for now. There is no Android version yet.

What if I forget to check in for a few days?

That's fine. Mầm never counts streaks and never takes points away. You can check in for earlier days, or just carry on from today.

What happens when I change phones?

In Settings → Your data, choose Export data to get a backup file, then Import data on the new phone. Mầm checks the whole file and asks whether to merge or replace before writing anything.

Or turn on iCloud sync on both phones (same iCloud account) and tap “Sync now” on the old phone first, then the new one.

Is Mầm a replacement for therapy?

No. Mầm is a personal journal and reminder tool, not a substitute for medical or psychological advice. “Need support?” in Settings lists emergency numbers in Vietnam.

Which languages does it support?

Vietnamese and English; you can switch in Settings.
